Week 1

Explore · Research · Design

3 Jul – 10 Jul
T-01

Hostel Audit & Photo Inventory

Research Design
📅 Deadline: Saturday 4 July, 14:00

Steps

  • Walk every floor (4 floors) and photograph every room, corridor, common area, bathroom and stairwell.
  • Note the dimensions of blank wall surfaces (approximate is fine).
  • Identify surfaces suitable for vinyl: smooth, paint-ready, no humidity damage.
  • Identify spots for 3D-printed objects: shelves, reception desk, common areas.
  • Write a short paragraph per floor: "What's missing? What's the mood? What would YOU want to see here as a traveller?"
  • Interview 2–3 hostel guests about what decor they've seen in hostels they loved.
📁 What to upload A PDF audit report with embedded photos. One section per floor. Include a summary table: Surface / Location / Size / Type (vinyl/3D/other) / Priority (High/Med/Low).
Drive / Week1 / T01_hostel_audit/

💡 Tip Use your phone or a camera in portrait AND landscape. Good lighting = better design references. Morning light is best.
T-02

Brand Mood Board & Visual Identity

Design Strategy
📅 Deadline: Wednesday 8 July, 14:00

Steps

  • Research the Canary Islands visual identity: volcanic rock, ocean gradients, banana plants, Teide, surfing culture, petroglyph symbols from the Guanche people.
  • Look at 5 competing hostels on Instagram — analyse their visual style.
  • Create a mood board in Canva, Figma, or Adobe: 20+ images, textures, colour swatches, typography.
  • Define a colour palette (max 5 colours) and 2 font pairings for the hostel brand.
  • Write a 150-word "brand voice" statement: How should Canary Campus feel? Sound? Look?
📁 What to upload Mood board PDF (A3, landscape). Colour palette file (hex codes). Brand voice statement (.txt or .pdf). Competitor analysis table.
Drive / Week1 / T02_moodboard/

💡 Tip Pinterest, Behance, and Instagram saved posts are your friends. Look up "hostel interior design 2024" and "Canary Islands aesthetics".
T-03

Vinyl Decal Designs (Silhouette Curio)

Design Print
📅 Deadline: Friday 10 July, 14:00

Steps

  • Based on T-02 mood board, design at least 6 vinyl decals in vector format (SVG or Silhouette Studio file).
  • Design variety: 2 typographic (quotes, hostel name, WiFi password styled beautifully), 2 illustrative (Canary Islands motifs), 2 functional (room numbers, wayfinding icons).
  • Match each decal to a specific wall/location from the T-01 audit.
  • Choose vinyl colours that complement the current white walls.
  • Do a test cut on scrap vinyl before final cuts.
📁 What to upload All vector files ready to cut. A placement map PDF showing which decal goes where. Photo of at least one test cut.
Drive / Week1 / T03_vinyl_designs/

💡 Tip Silhouette Studio has a "trace" function — you can import a PNG logo and auto-trace it to a cut path. Also: thicker vinyl sticks better on textured walls.
T-04

3D Object Design (Bambu Lab A1)

Design 3D Print
📅 Deadline: Friday 10 July, 14:00

Steps

  • Design or source at least 4 objects to 3D print for the hostel.
  • Ideas: decorative wall tiles with Guanche petroglyph patterns, phone holders, cable management clips, a custom reception bell, plant pots, key fobs, a hostel logo sign.
  • Model original objects using Tinkercad, Fusion 360, or Blender — or customise open-source models from Printables.com.
  • Prepare files in .3mf or .stl, sliced in Bambu Studio. Check print time estimates.
  • Start printing in Week 1 for pieces that take longer — coordinate overnight prints.
📁 What to upload .3mf or .stl files for all objects. Screenshot of Bambu Studio slice preview per object. A description card for each object (what it is, where it goes, filament colour).
Drive / Week1 / T04_3d_objects/

💡 Tip Bambu Lab A1 is fast — 20cm objects can print in 2–3h. Plan prints to run while you work on other tasks. PLA+ is the most versatile filament.

Vinyl Installation — Floors 1 & 2

Print Design
🔴 Deadline: Thursday 16 July, 14:00

Steps

  • Clean all target wall surfaces with a damp cloth and let dry completely (24h before applying).
  • Apply vinyl decals using the wet method or dry method depending on surface type.
  • Use a squeegee or old credit card to remove air bubbles.
  • Photograph each installed decal: one wide shot (context) + one close-up (detail).
  • Note any issues (surface didn't stick, size too big/small) in an installation log.
  • Install at least 3 decals on Floors 1 and 2 this week.
📁 What to upload Before/after photos per wall. Installation log (.txt or .pdf). At least 1 video of the installation process (15–30s, for Reels).
Drive / Week2 / T06_vinyl_install/

💡 Tip Work in pairs — one to position, one to press and smooth. Don't apply in direct sunlight (vinyl gets too sticky). Morning or evening is best.

Audio
